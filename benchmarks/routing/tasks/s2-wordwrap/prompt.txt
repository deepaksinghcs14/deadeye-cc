Create a new Go package at internal/wordwrap/wordwrap.go (package wordwrap) with `func WordWrap(text string, width int) []string`. Greedily wrap the text into lines of at most `width` RUNES (count runes, not bytes) without splitting a word across lines. Collapse any run of whitespace between words to a single space when joining. A word longer than `width` goes on its own line, unsplit. Lines must have no leading or trailing spaces. Input that is empty or all-whitespace returns an empty slice (length 0).
